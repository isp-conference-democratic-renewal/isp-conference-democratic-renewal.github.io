Democratic Decline and Renewal · short essays for circulation by March 24, 2027

Each panel addresses a specific theme, and we ask every contributor for a short essay of 1,000 to 1,500 words on it. The essay should identify what could change, who could act, and how the proposal would work. The essays are circulated to the panelists ahead of the conference so that contributors can see the questions they share, and, with your permission, they will later be published as an online symposium on Balkinization.

Submit yours below as a Word or PDF file, at any time between now and March 24, 2027. The file is kept exactly as you send it. You may send a revised version whenever you like; the latest one is the one we circulate.
