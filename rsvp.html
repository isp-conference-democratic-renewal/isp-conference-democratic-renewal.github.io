Democratic Decline and Renewal

The Information Society Project’s 2027 spring conference

Dear Colleague,

The Information Society Project at Yale Law School would be delighted to have you as a speaker at its 2027 spring conference, Democratic Decline and Renewal, to be held at Yale Law School in New Haven on April 2 and 3, 2027. This conference is part of the ISP’s new Constitution and Rule of Law (CAROL) Project. It asks how our institutions can be reformed to renew constitutional democracy and revive its fundamental values, and it brings academics, activists, and policy practitioners together to develop concrete reforms for democratic renewal. Each panel takes up one theme, and each panelist is asked to identify what could change, who could act, and how a proposal could work.

We ask every contributor for a short essay of 1,000 to 1,500 words by March 24, 2027. The essays are circulated in advance and, with your permission, later published as an online symposium on Balkinization. You can submit yours at any time through the Submit your essay tab at the top of this page. Please let us know whether you can join us by completing the form below.

Sincerely,

Jack M. Balkin, Genevieve Scott, Caleb Yong, Laura Matta and Henry Bradley
Yale Law School

This is an invitation-only, closed-door event. The invitation is not transferable; please let us know if there are colleagues you would like us to include.
